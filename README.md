# Arva Flour Mills — Our Story

Public history timeline of Canada's oldest commercial flour mill, on the banks of Medway Creek in Arva, Ontario since 1819.

Historical research by graduate students of Western University's Digital Public History course (April 2026) under the direction of Mike Dove, Director of Western Public History. Historical records held at Archives and Special Collections, Western Libraries (Fonds AFC526 — Arva Flour Mill).

## Deployment

Static site — HTML, CSS, and image assets. Served on Railway with the `serve` package.
